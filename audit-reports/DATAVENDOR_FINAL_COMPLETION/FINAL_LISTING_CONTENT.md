## OneLife Hospital Management

OneLife is an Express, EJS, and MongoDB source project for appointment booking, clinician scheduling, administration workflows, support conversations, online-consultation coordination, and payment/webhook integration points.

### Included engineering work

- Original Admin and Doctor management interfaces with responsive, keyboard-aware controls.
- Role-based browser workflows for patients, doctors, and administrators.
- Availability calendars, appointments, support threads, waitlists, payment reconciliation hooks, and video-consultation lifecycle support.
- Local automated tests and deployment/setup documentation.

### Buyer disclosure

This is source code for owner review and staging validation, not a certified healthcare platform or a production deployment. External provider credentials, data, hosting, legal terms, and final compliance are not included. The sale package must exclude historical restricted template material and unresolved assets. The bundled country/state/city data is separately noticed under ODbL-1.0 and remains subject to legal review of attribution/share-alike treatment.
